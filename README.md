# Alex's Birthday Adventure (07)

Build a complete, polished, mobile-first interactive birthday greeting web app called “Alex’s Birthday Adventure” for my sister.

Her real name is Kriti and her nickname is Alex. The experience should feel like a beautiful interactive digital birthday present rather than a normal website.

CORE EXPERIENCE

The entire website should feel:

Magical

Warm

Emotional

Playful

Premium

Cinematic

Very interactive

Extremely smooth on mobile

Personal and memorable

It should be designed primarily for a mobile phone in portrait mode.

Do NOT make it look like a generic birthday template.

Use a premium visual style with:

Deep midnight purple background

Soft pink

Lavender

Champagne/golden highlights

White glowing elements

Glassmorphism cards

Soft gradients

Subtle stars and particles

Elegant shadows

Smooth animations

Beautiful typography

Lots of breathing space

Avoid excessive clutter.

The experience should progressively reveal itself like a small interactive birthday story/game.

SCREEN 1 — OPENING / GIFT

When Alex opens the website, show a beautiful dark dreamy screen.

Background:

Animated tiny stars

Floating particles

Very subtle glowing gradients

Occasional floating hearts/sparkles

In the center, show an elegant glowing birthday gift box.

Text:

“Hi, Alex ✨”

Then:

“Happy Birthday, Kriti”

Small subtitle:

“A tiny adventure has been prepared for you…”

Below it:

TAP TO OPEN YOUR PRESENT 🎁

The button should have a soft glowing animation.

When she taps it:

Gift opens with a beautiful animation

Screen transitions into the birthday adventure

Start soothing background music

Small burst of particles/confetti

Smooth cinematic transition

Music should be soft, emotional and dreamy — NOT loud or childish.

If browser autoplay restrictions prevent audio, start the music after the first tap.

Include a small music toggle button throughout the experience.

CHAPTER 1 — BALLOON MEMORIES 🎈

Title:

“Chapter One”

Main title:

“A Sky Full of Little Memories”

Subtitle:

“Pop the balloons. Every balloon is hiding something for you.”

Gameplay:

Balloons should continuously float upward from the bottom of the screen.

Use different beautiful pastel colors.

They should move at slightly different speeds.

Add tiny strings.

Balloons should gently sway.

User taps a balloon to pop it.

Popping should have a satisfying animation and tiny particle burst.

Play a subtle pop sound.

Each popped balloon reveals a small message in a beautiful floating glass card.

Example messages:

“You make ordinary days feel special. 💗”

“Keep the moments that make you laugh way too hard.”

“May life surprise you with beautiful things. ✨”

“Never forget how loved you are.”

“More adventures. More stories. More YOU.”

“Some people make a room brighter just by being there.”

After approximately 8–10 balloons are popped:

Show:

“Memory collection complete ✨”

Then unlock:

NEXT CHAPTER →

Do not allow the next chapter button before completing the activity.

CHAPTER 2 — AIM FOR THE STARS 🏹

Title:

“Chapter Two”

Main title:

“Aim For The Stars”

Subtitle:

“Your dreams aren't targets. They're destinations. But let's practice aiming anyway.”

Create a mobile-friendly bow-and-arrow interaction.

The user should:

Touch and hold the bow string.

Drag backward.

See the string stretch.

Release.

Arrow shoots forward.

Arrow should hit targets.

Targets should appear at different positions.

Use approximately 6 targets.

Targets can contain subtle labels such as:

“Dreams”

“Adventure”

“Success”

“Peace”

“Courage”

“Happiness”

When each target is hit:

Target breaks/glows

Particle animation

Small message appears

Messages should relate to dreams:

“Keep aiming higher.”

“Your dreams deserve a brave shot.”

“Small steps still count.”

“Don't shrink your dreams.”

“Future Alex is cheering for you.”

After all targets are hit:

“Every dream starts with one brave aim. ✨”

Unlock next chapter.

Make the controls feel natural on mobile.

CHAPTER 3 — THE WISH WHEEL 🎡

Title:

“Chapter Three”

Main title:

“The Wish Wheel”

Subtitle:

“Close your eyes for a second… pick a wish.”

Place a beautiful glowing circular wheel in the center.

Wheel should have multiple elegant sections.

Possible wishes:

“More laughter”

“A dream trip”

“Peace of mind”

“New adventures”

“Unexpected happiness”

“A brave new beginning”

“Everything you deserve”

“A year to remember”

Add a pointer at the top.

Button:

SPIN THE WISH WHEEL ✨

When tapped:

Wheel spins smoothly

Add subtle sound

Add glowing particles

Slowly decelerate

Stop on one wish

Then display:

“✨ Your wish: [WISH] ✨”

Below:

“Keep this one close.”

Allow approximately 3 spins.

After the final spin, unlock the next chapter.

CHAPTER 4 — CATCH THE SWEET MOMENTS 🍦

Title:

“Chapter Four”

Main title:

“Catch The Sweet Moments”

Subtitle:

“Some moments are too sweet to let fall.”

Gameplay:

A cute ice-cream container/cup should be controlled by finger movement.

Place the container near the bottom.

Ice creams fall from the top.

User moves the container horizontally.

Catch the falling ice creams.

Missed ice creams disappear.

Caught ice creams create satisfying particle effects.

Use:
🍦
🍨
🍧

Approximately 10–12 successful catches should be required.

Messages occasionally appear:

“One more happy moment. 🍦”

“Collect the good stuff.”

“Life is sweeter with laughter.”

“Save room for joy.”

After completion:

“Sweet moment collection complete! 🍨✨”

Then unlock next chapter.

Make this game smooth and responsive with touch controls.

CHAPTER 5 — CONNECT THE STARS ⭐

Title:

“Chapter Five”

Main title:

“Connect The Stars”

Subtitle:

“Some beautiful things only appear when you connect the little moments.”

Create a beautiful night-sky screen.

Place glowing stars around the screen.

The user must tap stars in the correct order.

As each star is tapped:

Star glows brighter.

A glowing line connects it to the previous star.

Soft particle effect appears.

Gentle sound plays.

The star positions should eventually create a large HEART shape.

At the end, the complete heart should glow beautifully.

Then show:

“Look what you made. 💗”

And:

“Beautiful things are built one little moment at a time.”

Add a subtle heartbeat/glow animation to the final heart.

Unlock next chapter.

CHAPTER 6 — THE BIRTHDAY CAKE 🎂

Title:

“Chapter Six”

Main title:

“Bake. Light. Wish.”

Subtitle:

“One final little ritual…”

Create a playful interactive cake-building activity.

Start with an empty area.

Show cake layers as draggable/tappable blocks.

User needs to build a birthday cake.

For example:

Bottom cake layer

Middle cake layer

Top cake layer

Frosting/decorations

When all layers are placed correctly:

“Your cake is ready! 🎂”

Then show:

PLACE THE CANDLE 🕯️

User taps/drags the candle onto the cake.

Then:

LIGHT THE CANDLE 🔥

Candle lights up with:

Realistic-ish flame animation

Soft glow

Warm light

Tiny particles

Display:

“Make a wish…”

Then instruct:

“Swipe across the screen to blow out the candle 💨”

Detect a strong horizontal swipe.

When the candle goes out:

Flame disappears

Screen briefly darkens

Then a beautiful burst of stars/confetti appears.

Play a soft magical sound.

Show:

“Wish sent into the universe. ✨”

Then:

“May every good thing you wished for find its way to you.”

Optimize heavily for:

iPhone

Android

Chrome mobile

Safari mobile

Use:

Touch events

Swipe gestures

Responsive sizing

No tiny buttons

No horizontal scrolling

Keep instructions short and visual.

Use smooth transitions between chapters.

Every chapter should feel like entering a new scene.

DESIGN QUALITY

The most important requirement is that the website should feel like a premium interactive birthday gift.

When Alex opens it, she should immediately think:

“Wait… someone actually made this for me?”

It should feel personal, surprising and emotionally warm.

Do NOT make every chapter look like the same screen with a different game.

Each chapter should have its own visual identity while still belonging to the same overall birthday universe.

Use subtle chapter transition animations.

Make the final result polished enough that I can send the link directly to my sister without needing to explain how to use it.

Before considering the project complete, test every interaction on a mobile viewport and make sure:

No buttons are cut off

No text overlaps

Touch interactions work

Swipe detection works

Games can actually be completed

Next chapter only unlocks after completion

Music starts after the first user interaction

Replay works

The experience works from beginning to end without refreshing
